Flux Atlas web app: attribution and licences
============================================

Everything in web/src is original work written for Flux Atlas (a clean-room rewrite; no code from
earlier versions of the project). The globe engine in web/src/globe/engine is the port of the globe
lab (labs/globe/src/engine). This file lists everything else that ships in the app's public/ folder or
is bundled into its build, where it came from, and the terms it comes with. Nothing is hotlinked at
runtime: every texture, map and font is served by the app itself.

2. Coastlines, borders and state lines (public/data/land-*, countries-*, admin1-lines.bin)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), 1:110m and 1:50m land and countries, and 1:10m
state and province lines. Natural Earth is in the public domain: "Made with Natural Earth. Free vector
and raster map data at naturalearthdata.com." The boundaries are Natural Earth's de facto lines and take
no position on disputed territory.

The files are copied unchanged from labs/globe/public/data (labs/globe/scripts/build-textures.mjs
downloads them).

admin1-lines.bin
  The state and province boundaries the globe draws once the camera is down in a region: Natural Earth
  1:10m "Admin 1 - States, Provinces", the lines file (ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces_lines), release
  v5.1.2 of the official vector repository, commit f1890d9f152c896d250a77557a5751a93d494776.
  Data page: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/
  Source file: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces_lines.geojson
  (SHA-256 1a1f30ccaaf4cc9c4bde34266f0b8cbb955d3a4cf254b756912255f2ec7c75b6; 21 MB, not part of this
  repository.)
  Changes: the lines are internal boundaries only, so the stretches that run along a coastline or a country
  border the globe already draws are cut away; parts of one boundary are joined; each line is sorted into
  one of four detail levels from Natural Earth's own MIN_ZOOM; lines are simplified (Douglas-Peucker,
  0.004 to 0.014 degrees by level), rounded to 0.001 degree and packed as delta-coded varints (312 KB, about
  277 KB gzip). web/scripts/borders-admin1.mjs rebuilds the file: it downloads the pinned release, checks
  the hash above and writes the same bytes every run. The file is fetched only when the camera comes down
  and never on the Lite level.
  Terms: public domain, as above.

4. The Flux symbol (public/brand/Flux_symbol-mark_blue.svg)
-----------------------------------------------------------

The official Flux symbol from the Flux media kit, copied unchanged from assets/brand/flux/symbol. It
is the shape of the "Flux moon" in the globe; its outline is compiled into
src/globe/engine/moon/symbol.ts. Flux, the Flux logo and the Flux symbol are trademarks of their
owners. The renderer follows the rules of the Flux Brand Book (v2.0) as summarised in
assets/brand/flux/README.md: the symbol is drawn only in Flux blue #2B61D1, white, black, gray or the
tonal blue of the brand guides, with its proportions unchanged, and no tier colour ever touches it. It
stands for the Flux chain in a Flux ecosystem tool; it is not the logo of this project.

6. The easter egg

The handlebar moustache drawn in light across the planet (src/globe/engine/ambient/egg.ts) is an
