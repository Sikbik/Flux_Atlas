The Flux network, live, as a place you can open.

A globe of every node with the chain's 30 second heartbeat running through it, and windows you open on top: nodes, apps, blocks, payments, analytics. The moon that orbits it is the Flux symbol, and it is the chain. Everything below is interactive HTML on the real tokens.

Numbers are real fixture values from 2026-09-30 19:46 UTC. The globe is a Canvas2D reference for the art-direction contract, not the production renderer.

The moon is the chain

Click the moon, or press M. A producer sends a beam up to it. The bar sets aside the development fund, then the moon fires three payouts down, small to large: the small hexagon to Cumulus, the big hexagon to Nimbus, the cap to Stratus.
