# Flux brand assets

Official Flux logo files, copied from the Flux media kit (`media-logos-master`) for use in Flux Atlas,
a Flux ecosystem tool. Flux, the Flux logo and the Flux symbol are trademarks of their owners.

Rules from the Flux Brand Book (v2.0, Jan 2021):
- Never display the logo in any color outside the brand color guides: Flux blue `#2B61D1`, white, black,
  and the gray tones shown in the book (including the tonal blue and gray symbol variants).
- Keep the clear-space and proportions shown on the book's "Logo Overview" page.
- The symbol "synthesizes three blocks representing decentralized node power but at the same time
  functioning as a whole block."

The full brand book PDF (22.9 MB) is not committed. It lives in the original media kit.

- `symbol/`: symbol mark and round symbol (blue, white, black, gray, two-tone variants)
- `logo/`: symbol + "Flux" wordmark lockups
- `subtracted/`: knock-out variants
- `fluxos/`: FluxOS cube marks (FluxOS product palette: green `#36c9a5`, light green `#aee9db`, grey `#56af99`)
- `nodes/`: Flux Nodes product marks
